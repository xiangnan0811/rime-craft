A. Clean & Minimal

Inspired by Tailwind/shadcn docs. White background, subtle borders, focused typography. Right-side TOC. Calm, professional feel.

Pros

  • Highly readable
  • Matches shadcn/ui components already in your project
  • Easy to maintain

Cons

  • May feel generic
  • Less visual personality

B. Warm & Inviting

Amber/warm tones, card-based layout, rounded corners, soft shadows. Friendly and approachable - great for newcomers to Rime.

Pros

  • Welcoming to beginners
  • Distinctive personality
  • Tip/warning callouts stand out

Cons

  • Warm tones may not suit a technical tool
  • More visual complexity

C. Developer-Centric Dark

Inspired by Vite/Vue docs. Dark by default, code-first, syntax-highlighted blocks with copy buttons. Feels like a dev tool.

Pros

  • Matches Rime's technical identity
  • Code blocks are the star
  • Feels modern and developer-friendly

Cons

  • Dark-only may alienate some users
  • Harder to read long prose
